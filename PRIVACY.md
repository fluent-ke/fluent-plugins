# Privacy policy

This policy covers the plugins and skills in this repository: Fluent AI Skills, Daily Brief, Fluent Marketing Tools, Fluent 3D and Fluent Motion. They are published by Fluent ([fluent.ke](https://fluent.ke)), a team based in Nairobi, Kenya.

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

## The short version

The plugins are instructions and scripts that run inside the AI tool you already use, on your computer or in that tool's own environment. Fluent does not run a server for them, and nothing you do with them is sent to Fluent. The one exception is feedback you choose to send us through our form.

## What Fluent collects

**Nothing by default.** The skills contain no analytics, tracking, telemetry or remote logging. They do not read your chat history or your AI tool's memory, and they do not send your files or conversations anywhere on their own.

**Feedback you choose to send.** The `about-fluent` skill in Fluent AI Skills can help you write feedback and gives you a prefilled link to our feedback form. Nothing is sent until you open the link, review the form and submit it yourself. The form collects:

- the skill you are writing about, the tool you use it in, and the type of feedback
- the feedback text you write
- your email address, only if you add it to get the changelog

The form is hosted by Zoho Forms, which processes it on our behalf. Zoho also records technical details of the submission, such as the time and your IP address. We use the form to fix and improve the skills, and we use your email address only to send the changelog you asked for. We keep feedback for up to two years, and keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

## What runs on your side

Some skills, at your request, use services that you choose and control. Fluent receives nothing from these services.

- **Daily Brief** researches public web sources on your schedule. If you set up email delivery, it sends the brief through your own email account, to recipients you list, using a password kept in your computer's own keychain. On the Cowork route it can read the email, calendar and drive accounts you connect, and save the brief as a draft in your mailbox.
- **Fluent Marketing Tools** works through the Pencil and Meta Ads connections you install in your AI tool, under your own accounts.
- **Fluent 3D** installs and drives apps on your computer, including the open-source FreeCAD MCP add-on, which listens only on your own computer.
- **Fluent Motion** installs Playwright from npm into your project folder and its Chromium browser from Playwright's download servers, downloads a font from GitHub, and renders video on your computer. Any voice or music service you choose to use is under your own account, and receives the script or prompt you give it.

Each of those services has its own privacy policy, and that policy applies to what you send it.

## Your choices

You can uninstall any plugin at any time. To see, correct or delete feedback you have sent us, or to unsubscribe from the changelog, email learn@fluent.ke.

## Children

The plugins are made for adults using AI tools at work. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## Changes

We update this page when the plugins change what they do with data. The date at the top tells you when it last changed, and the repository history shows every change.
