# Content specs — what each format must communicate

A content spec is the **ranking**, settled once per format so it is not re-derived on every job. It names slots and their order. It never names words, and it is not a layout.

**Answer the spec before writing a single line of copy.** Copy written before the slots are agreed is copy arguing for its own existence, and it is the most expensive kind to throw away.

**The format decides what must be communicated. It does not decide how you decide.** Everything in the parent skill — segments, rhythm, optical resolution, anchoring, device scarcity — applies unchanged to every format below.

---

## Event poster

| Rank | Slot | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Identity** | Which event this is, in whatever form the audience already recognises. |
| 2 | **Promise** | What someone walks out with. |
| 3 | **When** | Date and time. |
| 4 | **Where** | Online, or in person and the venue. **In-person events only** — omit it entirely when there is nowhere to go. |
| 5 | **Cost** | Price, or that it is free. |
| 6 | **Entry** | The single next action. One only. |

**Anything past the last rank moves to the channel that carries it** — the caption, the landing page, a second asset. The agenda, the argument, the proof, the speaker list, the who-it's-for.

**When and Where are not detail; they decide whether someone can come at all.** They are the two ranks most often shrunk in the name of restraint, and shrinking them is withholding rather than taste. A premium price may be set quietly — a date and a venue may not. If someone has to enlarge the image to find out where it is, the poster has failed at a job the caption cannot do for it, because the image travels without the caption.

**Where the format has no such channel, the ranking is longer and the asset carries more.** A printed flyer, a poster pinned in a shop, an image forwarded on WhatsApp with no caption travelling beside it — these have no overflow, so cutting to five ranks throws information away rather than relocating it. Extend the spec, accept more segments, and lean harder on size and space to keep the order legible. Do not import an in-feed spec into a format that has nowhere to put the remainder.

**Which of 1 and 2 leads is a separate decision and it changes the poster.** Identity-led sets the name at title size and lets the promise convince underneath; it works when the audience already recognises the name and wastes the slot when they don't. Promise-led inverts it and carries a stranger further, but forfeits recognition you may already own.

**The logistics ranks usually resolve into one or two segments, not four.** Merging them is normal and often better than four thin blocks. Where the brand allows small functional icons, one per line — a calendar, a pin, a ticket — carries the merge without adding a device to each segment, because the icons read as one repeated system rather than as separate marks.

## Carousel

A carousel communicates at **two levels**, and both need a spec.

**The set** carries one message across all slides. Rank it as a whole: what someone knows after the last slide that they didn't after the first.

| Rank | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1 | The hook — the reason to swipe once |
| 2 | The turn — the thing that changes their mind |
| 3 | The payoff |
| 4 | Entry — one action, on the final slide only |

**Each slide** then carries one idea and one only, and slides share a single structure so the set reads as one object rather than a folder of images. A slide that needs two ideas is two slides.

## Thumbnail

| Rank | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1 | The one thing, in 2–4 words |
| 2 | A face or subject, if the content has one |
| 3 | Identity, small |

Nothing else survives at thumbnail scale. **Test it at 20% and check that rank 1 is still legible** — if it isn't, it is the only thing you are allowed to enlarge.

---

## Social post (single image, in-feed)

Same spec as the event poster minus rank 3 where there is no date, with one difference: **the caption carries the argument, so the image carries only the offer.** Anything that explains, persuades or elaborates moves to the caption.

---

## Adding a format

Write its spec **after** the format has been worked for real, not in advance. A guessed spec is a spec nobody follows. Capture it as: the ranks, what is caption, and which rank leads by default.
