# App routes

Routes that worked on a real iPhone (iOS 26, mirroir). Coordinates are left out on purpose: read them from the screen each time.

## Voice Memos: read a transcript

iOS transcribes recordings itself. A speech-bubble icon beside a recording in the list means a transcript exists, so no transcription tool is needed.

1. Open Voice Memos (from the App Library's Utilities group when it is not on the Home Screen).
2. Tap the recording, then its waveform icon to open the full view.
3. Tap the speech-bubble button at the bottom left. The transcript shows in place of the waveform.
4. The transcript does not scroll by swiping; it follows playback. Tap play and read the screen every 10 to 15 seconds until the end. Readings lag the audio by a few seconds.

The full view is the editor. Its red button reads **Replace** or **Resume**, and both record over the memo, so leave it alone and close with the checkmark. The **…** menu's **Copy Transcript** copies on the phone, but the copy may never reach the computer. For long recordings, send the audio files to the computer and transcribe them there instead.

## Instagram

- **DMs**: the paper-plane tab in the bottom bar. Read the list with a text query for the person's name, then tap it.
- **A reel someone sent**: tap it in the chat to open it full screen. It plays with sound. Tap the caption to expand the full text.
- **Search**: the magnifier tab, then tap the search field and type the handle. Results list near-duplicate handles; check them as SKILL.md describes.
- **A profile's posts**: tap the first grid tile to open the feed view, newest first. Each post's like, comment and share row sits under its media, with the caption and date below.

## Notes

- Opening the app shows the last note you viewed, not the list.
- The new-note button is at the bottom right; a new note opens with the cursor ready. Done is the checkmark at the top right.
- Typing multi-line text makes `type_text` report a mismatch because of line wrapping. Check the note by reading the screen instead.
